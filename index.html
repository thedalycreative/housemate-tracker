📋House Rules

Agreed at our first house meeting on 29 April 2026. Updated at every meeting.

💬 Communication

  • iMessage for house matters only - one topic at a time
  • Messenger for casual banter
  • 😀 Use emojis to indicate tone (helps Nessa read mood)
  • ⏳ 24-hour cool-off when emotional - tell the others first ("I need space")
  • Take turns speaking, don't interrupt
  • If a chat is unreadable, don't flood it with voice-dictated messages

🧹 Cleaning & Home

  • Cleaning split by ability, not by day
  • Nessa: washes, dries, puts away dishes; own laundry
  • Others: vacuuming, anything needing eyesight
  • 🪓 Tim to research a fortnightly cleaner (split 3 ways)
  • 📷 Google Home camera on during cleaner visits
  • 🔒 Lock doors. Block the cellar opening behind the red couch

🤝 Meetings

  • 📅 Weekly on Tuesdays at 6:30 pm with Auslan interpreter
  • Cancel with 2+ days notice (interpreter funding)
  • One topic at a time, park or resolve before moving on
  • All 3 sign off on agreements
  • ✅ Next meeting: 19 May 2026

📍 Future Plans

  • ✈️ Nessa back from Bali: 12 May 2026
  • 🏠 Tim & D'Arcy aiming to move June - August 2026
  • Nessa to start house-hunting once back from Bali
  • Plenty of warning to each other - no one left high and dry
